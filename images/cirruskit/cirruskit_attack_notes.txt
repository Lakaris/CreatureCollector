25 transparent 512 x 512 frames, arranged in a 5 x 5 grid. Read left to right, top to bottom. Each frame lasts 16 ms, for a 400 ms one-shot animation. This fits the game's existing 400 ms attack-art window. The first and final frames are exact copies of cirruskit_base.png.

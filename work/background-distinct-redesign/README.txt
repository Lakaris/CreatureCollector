Four redesigned creature backgrounds

Glass Beach: cool silver-mauve sand, a sweeping open coastline, and small sea-glass pebbles.
Volcanic Ridge: stepped basalt ridges and geothermal steam, with sparse ember fissures.
Snow Ghost Ridge: wind-sculpted, fully rime-covered trees above a mountain valley.
Open Plains: treeless green-and-gold prairie with a shallow winding stream.

Final files are 1536 x 1536 opaque JPG, exported at JPEG quality 85.
The corresponding images/flair/backgrounds files in the game were replaced after review.
Originals are retained in originals/.

Generated and edited using built-in ImageGen. Initial prompts are in prompts.json;
the two finishing edit prompts are in refinement-prompts.json. Full generation provenance
and selected outputs are recorded in generation-history.json.

previews/game-preview.jpg overlays a creature at approximate screenshot scale for review.
previews/comparison.jpg shows the unchanged comparison background, previous asset and redesign.
The actual background JPGs contain no creature.

Each scene has an open lower-central standing area. The preview uses bottom-aligned
cropping at the user's screenshot aspect ratio; it is a composed preview, not a live game capture.
